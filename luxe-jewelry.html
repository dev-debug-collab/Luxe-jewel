Our Story

Crafted with Love
Worn with Pride

A decade of passion, artistry, and dedication to creating jewelry that moves with you through life's most precious moments.

💎
Since 2015

Born From a Love
of Beautiful Things

Luxe Jewelry Collection was founded in 2015 by Aisha Malik, a passionate jewelry designer who believed that every woman deserves to feel extraordinary — every single day. What started as a small workshop in Lahore has grown into Pakistan's most beloved fine jewelry brand.

Each piece in our collection is thoughtfully designed, carefully crafted, and quality-tested to ensure it meets our exacting standards. We source only the finest materials — ethically, responsibly, and with deep respect for our artisans.

Today, with over 12,000 happy customers and a team of 40+ dedicated craftspeople, Luxe continues its mission: to make exceptional jewelry accessible to every woman who deserves to shine.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment information when you place an order. We also automatically collect certain information about your device and how you interact with our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to process your orders, send order confirmations and updates, provide customer support, send promotional communications (with your consent), and improve our website and services.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction. All payment information is encrypted using industry-standard SSL technology.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@luxejewelry.pk or +92 300 1234567.

Terms & Conditions

Order & Payment

By placing an order, you confirm that all information provided is accurate. Orders are subject to product availability. We reserve the right to cancel orders if we detect fraudulent activity. Cash on Delivery orders must be paid in full upon delivery.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for items in their original, unworn condition with all original packaging. Custom or personalized items are not eligible for return. To initiate a return, contact us at returns@luxejewelry.pk.

Shipping Policy

Standard delivery takes 2–5 working days across Pakistan. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available for an additional fee. Free shipping on all orders over Rs. 2,500. We are not responsible for delays caused by courier services or unforeseen circumstances.

Product Descriptions

We make every effort to display our products accurately. Colors may vary slightly due to photography lighting and screen display differences. All dimensions and weights listed are approximate.
